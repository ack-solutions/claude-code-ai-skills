# Usability evaluation

Use existing participant research when provided, respecting its scope and consent. Contacting or recruiting people is a separate action requiring user authorization.

For study planning:

- Identify the target audience and the uncertainty to test.
- Write neutral tasks describing goals without naming the intended button or teaching the solution.
- Choose relevant participant characteristics, device/locales and realistic starting conditions.
- Define observations such as unaided completion, errors, recovery, time on task and understanding of price/trust/consent labels.

For analysis:

- Separate direct observations and participant statements from interpretation.
- Report sample size and conditions. Small qualitative studies reveal problems but do not establish population conversion rates.
- Link findings to the affected journey and propose an improvement plus a way to test it.
- When only an AI/expert walkthrough is available, label likely friction as a hypothesis and report that no participant study was performed.
