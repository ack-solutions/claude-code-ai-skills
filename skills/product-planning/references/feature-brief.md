# Feature brief outline

Adapt to the project's format. Omit irrelevant sections; do not create empty documentation scaffolds.

- **Problem and audience:** whose task is difficult, the evidence and the desired outcome.
- **Scope:** included behaviour, excluded behaviour and dependencies.
- **Journey:** actor, starting state, action, outcome and recovery for important alternatives.
- **Rules:** states/transitions, ownership, permissions, limits, duplicate actions and data lifecycle.
- **Interfaces:** affected screens, API/events and external integrations. Record contract proposals without mistaking them for implemented behaviour.
- **Quality:** accessibility/locales/devices, latency or capacity goals, compatibility and operational expectations relevant to this feature.
- **Acceptance:** observable Given/When/Then cases, including failure and forbidden actions.
- **Measurement:** task completion and business outcome, proposed events, available baseline and measurement limits. Never invent a baseline.
- **Decisions:** decided, proposed or open; who or what evidence can settle a material uncertainty.
- **Delivery:** useful increments with acceptance criteria and dependency order.

Keep research evidence separate from decisions: a competitor feature can motivate a proposal but does not automatically become a requirement.
